# Reference: "Claude Edit" explainer reel (Instagram, Pradj abdllhisn)

https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dega0LsBOnR/ — before/after of a MrBeast interview clip ("we work on videos 3–4
months in advance…"). 24.2s, 1080×1920, one shot + one jump cut, 238 words/min, −14 LUFS / −0.8 dBTP.
`stills.jpg` = the edit side at 2.5, 6, 8.5, 11, 12.5, 14, 17, 19.5, 21, 23.3s; `details.jpg` = mid-animation frames.

What makes it work: a new graphic on almost every number or idea (every ~2s), each built from the transcript, one
idea at a time, in one consistent system.

## System
- Accent lime **#C8FC3C** (measured), white text, near-black cards **#05080B**, light cards **#F4F5F5**.
- One geometric grotesk (Inter Tight / Geist-like), bold for numbers and titles; small caps-mono-ish labels with wide
  tracking (START, UPLOAD, FINISH, IN ADVANCE).
- Line icons in lime (lucide-style: calendar, brain, lightbulb, hourglass, flag, upload, video camera, scissors, banknote).
- Captions: small (≈30px at 1080w) white sentence case, bottom (y≈1450), key word in lime; sometimes on a dark
  translucent rounded chip.

## Scene kinds seen
1. **Side stat** next to the head: icon + small label ("we work on videos"), huge number ("3–4"), unit ("months"),
   lime chip ("IN ADVANCE"). Right of the face, top third.
2. **Word highlight**: "idea|tion" letters blur in one by one with a few lime spark dots, then a lime highlighter block
   swipes behind "idea"; icon above, subtitle below ("where we brainstorm"). Chest height.
3. **Timeline**: START flag ● ——— ● UPLOAD marker, the line fills lime left→right; label above ("1–2 months") resolves
   with a text-scramble decode.
4. **Full-screen dark card**: lightbulb icon, kicker "coming up with", title "the idea", lime value "3–4 months",
   progress bar filling; captions keep running underneath.
5. **UI chips** stacked top-right: [camera] Filming · a couple weeks / [scissors] Editing · 3 weeks, dark glass, lime icon.
6. **Full-screen light card**: hourglass, "so probably", giant "5", "months", START ●——○ FINISH line; caption in a chip.
7. **Money counter** over the chest: chip "2 years ago", digits roll "$85,320" → "$100,000".
8. **Dark card counter**: old value struck through ("$100K"), new value counts up huge in lime ("$0.7M" → "$1.5M").

## Motion
- In: 0.25–0.35s (blur + rise / per-letter stagger / scramble); hold while the line is said; out fast (0.2s) or hard cut
  to the next scene. Numbers always count or roll, never just appear. Progress bars fill over the spoken duration.
